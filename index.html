Fig. I  —  The case, opened

Strip the case away, and what remains is pure engineering.

AK-01 is a hand-wound skeleton tourbillon built from 211 parts and 31 jewels — nothing tucked behind a dial. Keep scrolling and we dismantle it in the exact order it was assembled.

Fig. II  —  Exploded along the axis

Twelve layers. 6.1 millimetres thin.

Sapphire, hands, chapter ring, bridges, winding works, going train, tourbillon, barrel, mainplate, case. Each wheel turns at its real-world ratio — and the hands are telling your time, right now.

  • 32.4 mm calibre
  • 31 jewels
  • 211 parts

Fig. III  —  Energy

Hold the crown. Feel it wind.

Inside the drum sits 42 centimetres of coiled mainspring. Every turn of the crown tensions it around the arbor while the click holds it from unwinding. Thirty turns, three full days of power.

Press and hold the crown, or the gauge

Fig. IV  —  Transmission

Four wheels turn days into seconds.

The barrel completes one turn in eight hours. The centre wheel turns hourly and carries the minute hand. The third wheel multiplies the speed again, until the tourbillon cage rotates exactly once per minute.

  • 96 : 12 barrel
  • 80 : 10 centre
  • 75 : 10 third

Fig. V  —  Regulation  ·  shown at ⅒ speed

Eight beats a second, it lets go.

The pallet fork frees the escape wheel half a tooth at a time and returns a small impulse to the balance. The balance swings nearly three-quarters of a turn each way, timed by a hairspring finer than a human hair. The entire escapement rides in a cage turning once a minute, cancelling gravity out.

  • 4 Hz
  • 290° amplitude
  • 0.29 g cage

Finishing

Hidden surfaces get the same finish.

3hours on one movement

Côtes de Genève

Stripes are drawn across each bridge with a boxwood wheel — one pass per band, lined up bridge to bridge so the pattern reads as a single sheet of metal.

4,000hand-grained circles per mainplate

Perlage

Each circle is grained into the mainplate individually with a spinning peg. Together they scatter light beautifully — and trap the dust that would otherwise reach the pivots.

64hand-cut inward angles

Anglage

Edges are filed to forty-five degrees and polished with gentian wood until each one throws a single clean line of light. No machine can cut an inward angle. AK-01 carries sixty-four of them.

Reassembled

Built twice. Once to prove, once to keep.

Every AK-01 is assembled in full, regulated in six positions over three weeks, then stripped down for final finishing — and rebuilt for the last time by the same hands that started it.
